For a while I've had this quiet worry that ideas were slipping past me. I capture everything, but my notes live in a Google Keep pile with 400-something things in it that I almost never go back to. The idea I had in March turns out to be the missing half of the one I had in July, and I only ever notice by accident, if at all.

If your mind works a bit like mine, you probably know the feeling. I'd wanted to build something for it for a long time, so I finally did. It's called Sparkles, and it turns your notes into a night sky where you can actually see how ideas connect, instead of losing them at the bottom of a list.

I also used a lot of AI to build it. It saves real time, but only if you use it right. Left to guess, it'll undo a decision you made last week. Given a clear spec, it becomes an actual collaborator. That's the whole difference between vibe coding and building with intention, and it changed how I think about building things.
